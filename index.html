Three questions to ask every time

1. Was something really there? Yes, misread: an illusion. Nothing there: a hallucination.
2. Does she know it isn't real? Insight kept, often calm curiosity: think release phenomena from the brain or the eyes (brainstem lesions, vision loss, medication). Insight lost, with delusions: think primary psychosis, delirium or intoxication.
3. Which sense, and when? Visual, formed, evening or night: brain, eyes or drugs first. Voices commenting or commanding: primary psychiatric illness is more likely. Brief, stereotyped, with other signs: consider seizure or migraine.

The loop

Her therapist describes a cycle: a simple task overloads her, the slowing upsets her, the upset brings on physical symptoms, and losing control of her body upsets her more. Turn on treatments and see what weakens the loop.
